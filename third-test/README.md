# third-test: managerial leadership and truth-telling

Replication of Brandts and Cooper (2025), *Managerial Leadership, Truth-Telling, and Efficient Coordination*, The Economic Journal 135(670), 1942-1979, https://doi.org/10.1093/ej/ueaf019. Human values were recomputed from the authors' replication package.

## Design

A manager and two agents in seven treatments that vary the communication between them and the manager's decision rights.

## Results

- Replicated: chat outperformed the safe outcome, and agents lied less in chat than with structured reports.
- Not replicated:
  - With structured reports, agents misreported in 11.1% of late rounds, against 41.6% for humans.
  - Agent managers erred in 3.3% of decisions, against 25.1% for humans.
  - Structured reports already reached 78.5 of 80, so chat added 0.56 against 3.51 for humans.
- Diagnosis:
  - In pilots, late false reports appeared only at the highest reasoning effort.
  - Late false reports under structured reports rose from 5 with private notes to 17 without.
  - Transcripts were scanned for the authors' names.
  - 31 of the 32 recomputed human outcome values matched the paper; the discrepancies were recorded.
