# Research approach: synthetic organisations for theory building

## Project goal and paper idea

Our core idea is to use synthetic agents to build theoretical insights in organisational science. Synthetic organisations would provide an experimental setting for investigating organisational phenomena that are difficult to test in real life. The intended contribution is to explain organisational phenomena, identify mechanisms and boundary conditions, and develop theory. Synthetic agents are the research instrument.

The particular opportunity is counterfactual investigation. In real organisations, we observe one realised history. In a synthetic setting, we can construct matched versions of an organisational situation and vary its coordination arrangements, information distribution, or decision rights. This lets us investigate what might have happened under an alternative arrangement while keeping other specified features constant. Such experiments can generate theoretical propositions about when an organisational mechanism possibly operates, fails, or reverses.
