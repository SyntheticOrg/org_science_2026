Interpretation limits

Advocacy and cue labels are automated and provisional. Generated notes are not internal motivation. Cue reporting is calculated only for distributed information. Control advocacy uses the corresponding unpaid target roles; weak-buy and strong-buy coded counts use paid roles. A correct majority can coexist with private-target advocacy. Profitable unilateral deviations hold others' final votes fixed and do not identify beliefs or equilibrium selection.
