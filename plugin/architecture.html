Synthetic Organisations

Plugin architecture and research workflow

The plugin supports replication and counterfactual exploration with language-model agents. It connects explicit assumptions, study-specific construction, validity checks, and a traceable research record.

Research purposes

Replication

Examine whether a specified synthetic organisation reproduces a reference finding and the processes relevant to it.

Counterfactual exploration

Compare theoretically motivated alternatives, with claims scoped to the assumptions and evidence examined.

System architecture

Both hosts load the same six skills, written as Markdown instructions with supporting references and a study-plan template. The host assistant follows this guidance to help design and construct a study.

Research workflow

The workflow connects design decisions to observations and interpretation. Counterfactual comparisons may be planned from the outset or developed from earlier findings.

Validity considerations

Validity guidance considers social preferences, behavioural variation, memory and communication limits, and private-information disclosure. These are starting points; 18 supporting modules offer further considerations when relevant to the question.

Research record and reporting

The kit records run settings, prompt templates, file hashes, and launch events. The assistant uses these records with the study plan to support analysis and reporting.

GUIDE-LLM reporting support

14 items across seven reporting areas

The reporting reference maps these items to study evidence; the assistant drafts entries from available records. Validation, data handling, and competing-interest statements require evidence and researcher judgement; unavailable facts remain explicit.
