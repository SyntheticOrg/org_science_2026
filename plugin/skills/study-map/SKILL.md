# Study map

Use this optional helper when the researcher asks how the plugin works or where a study stands. Start new work through [design-study](../design-study/SKILL.md) and continue within existing authorisation without requiring a sequence of skill commands.

| Stage | Guiding question |
|---|---|
| **Design** | What is being investigated, which assumptions matter, and what evidence could examine them? |
| **Check** | Does the construction represent those assumptions, and what might undermine the interpretation? |
| **Run** | Which observations or comparisons address the question, and how will their specification be retained? |
| **Counterfactuals** | What might change under an alternative arrangement, which assumptions change, and what can the comparison support? |
| **Interpret** | What do the findings support, which alternatives remain, and where do the claims apply? |

Inspect the actual plan, implementation, and evidence, using the project's existing format. A missing template field or kit filename is not itself evidence of an incomplete study. A completed run or passing mechanical check does not establish scientific validity.

Recommend the next useful step:

- Clarify consequential assumptions and proposed evidence when the question or design remains unresolved.
- Use [construction guidance](../simulation-harness/SKILL.md) and [setup](../setup-synthetic-organisations/SKILL.md) as needed to realise the chosen design.
- Use [validity guidance](../validity-audit/SKILL.md) to examine uncertainties that matter to the intended claim.
- Carry out the planned observations with traceable settings and a clear distinction between adjustment and evaluation.
- For counterfactual exploration, formulate theoretically motivated alternatives, revisit relevant assumptions and checks, and compare outcomes and processes with the reference setting. Record whether these comparisons were planned or developed from earlier findings; a replication-only study can leave this step out of scope.
- Use [study protocol](../study-protocol/SKILL.md) to interpret findings, remaining assumptions, and claim scope.

The stages can be revisited. Explain why the next step is useful for this research question and record consequential changes.
