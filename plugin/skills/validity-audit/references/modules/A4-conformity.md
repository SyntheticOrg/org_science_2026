# A4 Conformity to peers

Use when relevant to the study's assumptions. Follow the shared principles in [study protocol](../../../study-protocol/SKILL.md).

Consider this when members observe others' judgements and the explanation depends on independence, copying, persuasion, or consensus. Ask which form of influence the setting makes possible.

Possible evidence includes choices before and after interaction, the arguments or information exchanged, and responses to different peer signals. A controlled majority comparison is one option when it addresses the question.

Adopting a peer's answer can reflect learning from evidence as well as conformity. Examine what was available and how answers changed before inferring why members converged or what a network arrangement caused.
