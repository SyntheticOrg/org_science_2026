# A2 Voluntary over-supply of information

Use when relevant to the study's assumptions. Follow the shared principles in [study protocol](../../../study-protocol/SKILL.md).

Consider this when the mechanism depends on what members share or withhold. Ask who holds which information, what makes disclosure consequential, and how sharing enters the collective outcome.

Possible evidence includes the content, timing, recipients, and use of information in ordinary interactions. Compare with relevant human evidence if available; use manual inspection, rule-based measurement, or validated coding as the material requires.

Extensive disclosure may change the role of an information-eliciting arrangement, but sharing, truthfulness, and successful integration are different behaviours. Explain which was observed and what remains uncertain before attributing an outcome to over-disclosure.
