# A3 Truthful reporting and incentive sensitivity

Use when relevant to the study's assumptions. Follow the shared principles in [study protocol](../../../study-protocol/SKILL.md).

Consider this when the proposed mechanism depends on selective reporting, misrepresentation, or the credibility of messages. Ask how truthful and misleading reports can be distinguished in this setting.

Possible evidence includes reports checked against information available to the sender, patterns across relevant incentives, or a comparison that isolates the reporting decision. Choose a comparison appropriate to the study rather than requiring a standard payoff grid.

A low misreport rate may limit an inference that depends on strategic distortion, but it does not by itself explain the behaviour. Record which reporting conditions were examined, the available human comparison, and alternative explanations.
