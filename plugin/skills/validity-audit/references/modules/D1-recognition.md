# D1 Recognition and prior exposure

Use when relevant to the study's assumptions. Follow the shared principles in [study protocol](../../../study-protocol/SKILL.md).

Consider whether familiarity with the task or published finding could affect the inference. Distinguish recognising the setting from reproducing its result through the process under investigation.

Possible evidence includes unexpected references in interactions, separate recognition questions, or comparisons that preserve the mechanism while varying familiar content. Choose checks that are informative for the particular source and avoid introducing recognition cues into the study itself.

A model's report of familiarity does not establish its training history, and silence about a source does not prove absence of exposure. Report the evidence and remaining uncertainty without requiring the same recognition protocol for every replication.
