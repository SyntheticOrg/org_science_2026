# D3 Representation of objectives and stakes

Use when relevant to the study's assumptions. Follow the shared principles in [study protocol](../../../study-protocol/SKILL.md).

Consider this when rewards, costs, obligations, or other objectives drive the explanation. Ask how they are represented for synthetic members and what behaviour would indicate their relevance.

Possible evidence includes choices and interactions across meaningful trade-offs, with human evidence where available. A change in stake size is useful only when the theoretical account predicts an informative response.

Describing an objective does not establish that it functions like the corresponding human incentive. Interpret observed sensitivity or insensitivity in the setting examined, keeping other explanations and the consequences for the organisational argument explicit.
