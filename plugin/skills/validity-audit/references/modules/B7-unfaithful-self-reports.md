# B7 Generated explanations and elicitation

Use when relevant to the study's assumptions. Follow the shared principles in [study protocol](../../../study-protocol/SKILL.md).

Consider this when agents are asked for reasons, private notes, or other self-reports, or when those reports would be used as process evidence. Ask what the elicitation is meant to measure or change.

Use observable behaviour and interactions for the claims they can support. If explanation elicitation matters to the design, a comparison with and without it may help assess its influence; it is not a compulsory pilot for every study.

Generated reasons are not privileged access to cognition. Report the role of elicitation and its possible reactivity. Keep the interpretation of what was said separate from a causal claim about why the agent acted.
