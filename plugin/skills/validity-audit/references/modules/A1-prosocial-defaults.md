# A1 Prosocial and cooperative defaults

Use when relevant to the study's assumptions. Follow the shared principles in [study protocol](../../../study-protocol/SKILL.md).

Consider this when the explanation depends on self-interest, cooperation, or responses to conflicting interests. Ask what motives are assumed and whether the observed choices support that account.

Possible evidence includes choices or interaction patterns under relevant incentives, compared with human evidence where available. A focused allocation or disclosure task may help when it isolates an unresolved question, but an individual response does not establish its transfer to an interacting organisation.

High collective accuracy alone does not identify prosociality. Distinguish the observed choices from honesty, misunderstanding, strategic reasoning, or other explanations. Changing motive instructions is a construction choice whose effect and claim scope need to be examined.
