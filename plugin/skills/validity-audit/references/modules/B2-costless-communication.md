# B2 Communication, time, and effort constraints

Use when relevant to the study's assumptions. Follow the shared principles in [study protocol](../../../study-protocol/SKILL.md).

Consider this when the mechanism depends on the cost or scarcity of communication, search, attention, or effort. Ask which activities are constrained in the organisation and how those constraints are represented.

Possible evidence includes the amount, timing, content, and consequences of activity, with relevant human comparisons where available. A message limit, action budget, or time cost is an option only when it represents the intended assumption.

Changing a communication constraint can alter who speaks, what is exchanged, and the opportunities for other actions. Examine those consequences rather than treating matched message counts as sufficient evidence of correspondence.
