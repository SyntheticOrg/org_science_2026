# A5 Deference to authority

Use when relevant to the study's assumptions. Follow the shared principles in [study protocol](../../../study-protocol/SKILL.md).

Consider this when hierarchy, expertise, advice, or delegated authority matters to the explanation. Ask how authority is represented and whether behaviour depends on the role, its information, or its incentives.

Possible evidence includes reactions to advice, challenges to decisions, or comparisons of the same information under different authority arrangements. Preserve meaningful features of the source setting when designing the comparison.

Compliance alone does not identify undue deference. State what could distinguish justified reliance, role expectations, and resistance or submission to authority, and limit conclusions to the conditions examined.
