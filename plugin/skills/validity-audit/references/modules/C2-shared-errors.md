# C2 Dependence among member judgements

Use when relevant to the study's assumptions. Follow the shared principles in [study protocol](../../../study-protocol/SKILL.md).

Consider this when the explanation depends on independent perspectives, correlated errors, influence, or aggregation. Ask which dependencies are present before and during interaction.

Possible evidence includes joint patterns of judgement, changes after exchange, and the distribution of collective outcomes. Extreme outcomes may motivate investigation, but a particular statistic or comparison is not required in every setting.

Convergence can arise from shared model tendencies, common information, communication, or the task itself. Examine which account the evidence supports and account for dependence in the analysis rather than assuming that separate agent instances are independent members.
