## Counterfactual exploration, when pursued

Explain the alternative arrangement and its theoretical motivation. What changes, which features should remain comparable, and which assumptions are preserved, changed, or introduced? Identify any further construction checks or evidence needed before comparing outcomes and processes with the reference setting.

Record which comparisons were planned in advance and which developed from earlier findings. State what the alternative changes beyond the conditions already examined. A replication-only study can leave this section out of scope.

## Evidence and interpretation

Explain which observations or comparisons address the question, how they will be measured, and which reference evidence is available. Choose the analysis unit and treatment of dependence from the design. State how uncertainty, failed attempts, and alternative explanations will affect interpretation.

Specify the checks needed to establish that construction and measurement implement the intended design. Select further behavioural diagnostics for consequential uncertainties. Make any distinction between evidence used for adjustment and evidence reserved for evaluation explicit.

State what the planned evidence could support about the specified simulation and human organisations. Identify assumptions and changed settings for which correspondence remains untested.

## Research record

Record the implementation and configuration choices needed to interpret the findings, with their rationale. Link to prompts, code, inputs, and run records rather than transcribing them. Keep a history of consequential changes and what evidence informed them. Preserve the specification associated with each reported result and identify exploratory or sensitivity analyses.
